plugboard is a fast, local-first database client for PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, ClickHouse, Cassandra, and Redis. It is designed for people who want a simple, dependable way to open and work with their data on their own computer. There are no accounts to create, no cloud sync to manage, and no telemetry watching what you do. It is just you and your data.

## 🚀 Getting Started

plugboard is made for everyday computer users who need to look at, search, and manage database information without a complicated setup. You do not need to be a programmer to get started. You do not need to create a login. You do not need to connect the app to a cloud service. You download it, open it, and begin working with your data.

The app is built around a local-first approach. That means the important work happens on your computer, not on a remote server that you have to trust with your information. Your database details stay with you. Your queries stay with you. Your day-to-day work stays with you.

plugboard works with several popular database types, so you can use one clean application instead of jumping between different tools. It is a practical choice for Windows users who want a fast desktop app for browsing tables, checking records, running queries, and managing local or remote databases.

When you open plugboard for the first time, you will see a clean starting screen. From there, you can connect to a database by entering the connection details you already use. ## 🔒 Privacy and Local-First Design

plugboard is designed around a simple idea: your data belongs to you. That is why the app does not ask for an account, does not sync your work to the cloud, and does not collect telemetry. Telemetry means the app would send information about how you use it back to the developers. plugboard does not do that

When you connect to a database, the connection details stay on your computer. When you run a query, the query runs from your computer to the database you chose. plugboard does not route your data through a third-party service. This local-first design gives you a clear, direct path between you and your data

For people who work with sensitive information, this matters. You do not have to wonder where your data is being stored or who can see it. The app is a tool on your desktop, not a service that holds your information
